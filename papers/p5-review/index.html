A Review of Artificial Intelligence in Pedestrian Evacuation Safety: Applications, Challenges, and Future LLM–XAI Ecosystems

Tong Lu,  Yifan Ding,  Yuxin Zhang*,  Weikang Xie,  Xinyan Huang*

Research Centre for Smart City Resilience and Fire Safety, Department of Building Environment and Energy Engineering — The Hong Kong Polytechnic University

Paper Figures

Fig. 7. Research perspective of AI technology during the lifecycle of evacuation — covering the pre-disaster phase (performance-based design and planning), the routine operational phase (daily safety monitoring), and the emergency response phase (real-time decision-making).

Abstract

With the continuous increase in urban complexity and population density, safe evacuation during emergencies has become an important research topic in public safety management. This study supports smart safety and resilient cities by systematically reviewing AI applications in pedestrian evacuation. Following the PRISMA 2020 protocol, a systematic search of Web of Science, Scopus, and IEEE Xplore (2015–2025) yielded 728 articles for bibliometric analysis and 163 studies for quantitative benchmarking. Representative methods and trends in AI for evacuation modelling, behaviour prediction, and intelligent decision-making are identified.

Key Findings

  • AI significantly improves adaptability, accuracy, and speed of evacuation simulations
  • Three structural bottlenecks identified: multi-source data heterogeneity, benchmark deficits, and algorithmic opacity creating a regulatory compliance gap
  • Future direction: a unified "AI for AI" ecosystem orchestrated by LLMs and Explainable AI (XAI)
  • Redefined Human-in-the-Loop paradigm for intelligent and resilient public safety systems
