Human-AI Interactive Framework for Smart Evacuation Safety Analysis in Large Infrastructures

Tong Lu,  Yuxin Zhang*,  Weikang Xie,  Xinyan Huang*

Research Center for Smart Urban Resilience and Firefighting, Department of Building Environment and Energy Engineering — The Hong Kong Polytechnic University

Paper Videos

Fig. 1. IEPTool — a human-AI interactive framework integrating an LSTM+GAN prediction engine with an LLM for interactive evacuation risk analysis and optimization guidance.

Abstract

The increasing scale and complexity of large urban infrastructures have led to greater pedestrian concentrations and higher risks of crowd-related incidents in emergencies. This study develops an Intelligent Evacuation Prediction Tool (IEPTool) with a human-AI interactive framework for evacuation prediction and safety assessment. The tool is powered by a deep-learning engine trained on a comprehensive evacuation-simulation database of 66 real-life architectural floor plans, including air terminals, exhibition centers, large stadiums, and stations. By integrating LSTM networks and GAN, key metrics — evacuation time, per-exit pedestrian flow rate, and dynamic pedestrian-density distribution — are predicted with over 90% accuracy. A large language model is then incorporated for interactive risk analysis, enabling intelligent safety assessments and optimization guidance through an integrated GUI.

Key Contributions

  • LSTM+GAN engine trained on 66 real floor plans, >90% accuracy
  • Predicts evacuation time, per-exit flow rate, and dynamic density fields
  • LLM-powered interactive risk analysis and optimization guidance
  • Unified GUI for fast, reliable safety evaluation of complex layouts
